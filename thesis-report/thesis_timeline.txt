THESIS TIMELINE — B-PEFT (Bayesian PEFT for Reliable Few-Shot Vision)
======================================================================

01 - 2026-04-21
Work - Initial pipeline built (frozen ResNet-18 + Bottleneck adapter +
  evidential/linear head) and demoed on a single toy episode.
Purpose - Pre-defence proof-of-concept for the proposal's core claim.
Result - Evidential head showed an OOD/uncertainty edge but was badly
  miscalibrated (ECE = 0.526).

02 - 2026-04-21
Work - Step 1: Calibration fix. Corrected the evidential loss (was
  cross-entropy-on-softplus instead of evidential MSE+KL; KL prior was
  disabled), switched ReLU->softplus, added KL annealing 0->0.5 over 200
  steps.
Purpose - Fix the two bugs causing the ECE=0.526 miscalibration.
Result - ECE 0.526 -> 0.167 (order-of-magnitude fix), accuracy parity
  with softmax, AUROC(evidential) > AUROC(softmax) by a wide margin.

03 - 2026-05-13
Work - Step 2: Refactored into a config-driven repo (src/ subpackages,
  YAML `extends` config system, scripts/train.py + scripts/evaluate.py
  as sole entry points, 28 pytest tests).
Purpose - Make the codebase reproducible and extensible before scaling
  up experiments.
Result - Step 1 reproduced via CLI within tolerance (+/-1% acc,
  +/-0.01 ECE); no dead code; 28/28 tests passing.

04 - 2026-05-16
Work - Step 3: Added W&B logging (online/offline/disabled modes) and a
  reproducibility harness (deterministic seeding, frozen 600
  test-episode seed list in configs/test_episodes.yaml).
Purpose - Enable tracked, comparable, byte-identical experiment runs
  going forward.
Result - Same config run twice -> byte-identical metrics.json; all
  future evaluation now shares one frozen 600-episode test set.

05 - 2026-05-21 to 2026-06-11
Work - Step 4: Real CIFAR-FS Bertinetto 64/16/20 split + true episodic
  meta-training (parameter-free prototype head replacing the toy linear
  head).
Purpose - Move off the single-episode toy demo onto the real 5-way
  k-shot benchmark the thesis is judged on.
Result - NEGATIVE. Evidential AUROC gap over softmax (+0.006) fell
  below the pre-registered 0.05 bar, and evidential was worse
  calibrated (ECE 0.344 vs 0.119). Escalation triggered -> Step 4.5.

06 - 2026-07-07
Work - Step 4.5 "Settle the science": added temperature scaling (Guo
  2017), energy score (Liu 2020) as fair baselines, real TinyImageNet +
  CIFAR-100-heldout near-OOD sets, and a real VAL-only (seeds
  10000-10099) hyperparameter sweep of the evidential loss.
Purpose - Re-test RQ2/RQ3 fairly before building more PEFT methods on
  top of a disputed Step 4 result.
Result - TIER 3. Evidential vacuity decisively beats both softmax
  scores (MSP, TS-MSP) on every OOD pool (+0.076 to +0.141 AUROC), but
  is only competitive (not clearly better) than the energy score,
  losing TinyImageNet-near by -0.013. Calibration still ~7x worse than
  TS-softmax; VAL sweep confirmed the ECE surface is flat (~0.285-0.296).

07 - 2026-07-21
Work - Step 5: Rewrote LoRA as true backbone-weight reparameterization
  (was a pooled-feature stub), added BitFit, Full-Fine-Tuning and
  Linear-Probe baselines; 8 configs trained and evaluated (4 adapters x
  2 heads, N=600).
Purpose - RQ1 breadth (more PEFT methods) + proposal §6's mandatory
  non-PEFT baselines.
Result - Evidential's OOD edge over softmax generalises across all 4
  adapter types (+0.010 to +0.134 AUROC). Full-FT overfits on 5-shot as
  predicted, demonstrating why PEFT is necessary.

08 - 2026-07-21
Work - Step 6: Adapter placement study (serial vs parallel bottleneck
  insertion inside ResNet-18 blocks); 4 configs.
Purpose - RQ1 primary - does adapter placement affect the
  accuracy/parameter tradeoff?
Result - Parallel placement is the Pareto winner (0.915 acc, 0.933
  far-OOD AUROC at 31.7K params), beating both post-pool bottleneck and
  Step 5's Full-FT.

09 - 2026-07-22
Work - Step 7: Added Gaussian-noise far-OOD pool and consolidated
  near-OOD (TinyImageNet) results on the Step 6 placement winner
  (parallel).
Purpose - RQ3 primary - broaden the OOD pool beyond SVHN/CIFAR-100.
Result - Evidential beats softmax MSP on every OOD pool tested, with a
  LARGER margin on near-OOD (+0.08 to +0.10) than far-OOD (+0.045 to
  +0.048).

10 - 2026-07-26
Work - Step 8: Added MobileNetV3-Small as a second backbone; 4 configs
  (post-pool + parallel bottleneck x 2 heads) on CIFAR-FS.
Purpose - RQ4 prep - proposal's second mandatory lightweight backbone.
Result - Accuracy nearly free on the smaller backbone (~0.90-0.91), and
  parallel placement becomes a strict Pareto win there (matches or beats
  post-pool with FEWER params).

11 - 2026-07-30
Work - Step 9: Added MiniImageNet as a second in-distribution dataset
  (frozen Ravi & Larochelle 64/16/20 split); 10 configs across 2
  backbones x 2 placements x 2 heads + linear-probe references.
Purpose - RQ2/RQ3 breadth - proposal's second mandatory ID dataset.
Result - Accuracy near-saturated (0.94-0.96), confirming a measured
  ImageNet-pretraining-overlap confound; bottleneck/parallel remained
  the best (adapter, head) combination.

12 - 2026-08-06
Work - Step 10: Full MVT (Model x View x Task) grid: 120 cells = (2
  backbones x 5 adapters x 2 heads x 2 datasets x 2 shot regimes, most
  at 3 seeds), ~36.3 GPU-hours on Kaggle T4.
Purpose - Answer all four RQs at statistical scale instead of single
  configurations.
Result - RQ1 CONFIRMED - parallel bottleneck wins 16/16 matched
  comparisons. RQ2 CONFIRMED NEGATIVE at scale - evidential ECE worse
  than softmax in 20/20 pairs. RQ3 SPLIT - crushes softmax scores
  (37-38/40 wins) but loses to the energy score in ~70% of cells,
  CORRECTING the earlier Step 4.5 "on par with energy" read.

13 - 2026-08-09
Work - Step 11: Efficiency + Pareto-frontier measurement (RQ4): params,
  FLOPs, GPU+CPU latency, peak memory for all 12 grid keys on a
  canonical Kaggle T4 session, plus real (trained-free) ViT-B/16 and
  DeiT-Tiny reference measurements.
Purpose - RQ4 - map the latency vs uncertainty-quality Pareto frontier
  for edge deployment.
Result - RQ4 ANSWERED - backbone choice drives latency (5.12x ResNet-18
  vs MobileNetV3-Small), adapter choice barely does (3.9-5.8%);
  evidential head is ~free at inference (1.29% overhead, below the
  5.91% noise floor). Two real bugs found and fixed during closeout: (1)
  a CUDA/CPU device-placement crash in an optional reference-backbone
  feature; (2) a SILENT bug where three plotting scripts picked the
  wrong (dev-laptop) latency profile over the canonical Kaggle one by
  dict-ordering accident, causing up to 47% error on individual
  Pareto/Table-8 cells before being caught and fixed.

14 - 2026-08-27
Work - RQ3 matched-budget experiment: 48 runs rebuilding bottleneck vs
  LoRA at an EQUAL trainable-parameter budget within each backbone
  (MiniImageNet 5-shot, 2 backbones x 2 budgets x 2 arms x 2 heads x 3
  seeds).
Purpose - Disentangle whether Step 10's finding (the calibration winner
  flips exactly when parameter ranking flips between backbones) was
  driven by parameter BUDGET or by backbone IDENTITY - the 120-run grid
  could not separate these two explanations.
Result - "Backbone-intrinsic" confirmed - fired in 3/4 pre-registered
  cells vs the budget hypothesis's 0/4. Equalising the budget left
  ResNet-18's calibration gap fully intact and roughly halved
  MobileNetV3-Small's already-tiny one. Bottleneck's accuracy (8/8) and
  near-OOD AUROC (8/8) advantage survives unchanged at matched budget -
  the RQ3 dissociation (accuracy/near-OOD follow architecture,
  calibration follows backbone) is restated, not explained (mechanism
  still open).
