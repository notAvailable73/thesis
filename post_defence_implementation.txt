CRITICAL OBSERVATION â READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE:
  The evidential ECE (0.526) is WORSE than softmax ECE (0.040).
  This is the OPPOSITE of the thesis claim.
  AUROC favours evidential (good), accuracy is fine, but calibration
  is broken. Step 1 below exists because of this single number.

  Likely causes (in order of likelihood):
    1. ECE is being computed on Dirichlet mean prob p = alpha/S for
       evidential but on softmax(logits) for the baseline â the
       evidential p collapses to near-uniform when total evidence S
       is small, inflating ECE. Check metrics.py + evaluate.py.
    2. KL weight too high â pushes alpha toward uniform â confident
       wrong predictions get punished, but correct predictions also
       lose sharpness.
    3. Single episode = noisy estimate; 1 episode * 75 query images
       cannot give a stable ECE.
    4. Evidence activation: Softplus on raw logits can saturate.
