How it decides

the honest-AI story

1 Few-shot enrollment

Each product becomes a prototype — the average of its reference-photo embeddings from a frozen CNN. No retraining.

2 Evidential confidence

Similarity to each prototype becomes Dirichlet evidence. Little evidence ⇒ high uncertainty ⇒ the system says "I don't know".

3 Honest vs. overconfident

The grey softmax bars stay confident even on unknown items. The blue evidential bars don't — that gap is the point.
