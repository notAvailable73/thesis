================================================================================
0.  PAPER-GROUNDED REASONING (per thesis_implementation_instructions §5)
================================================================================
Sources: ODD_paper_summaries.txt (OpenOOD near/far split; energy score) +
Malinin & Gales (Dirichlet Prior Networks) as summarised.

WHICH PAPERS / WHY
  - OpenOOD frames OOD as NEAR (semantic shift, same domain) vs FAR (covariate
    shift, different domain). Softmax-MSP is known to be easy on far-OOD and to
    DEGRADE on near-OOD; evidential/Dirichlet vacuity is expected to hold up on
    near-OOD -- this is RQ3.
  - Gaussian white noise is the extreme FAR-OOD end: if a model cannot separate
    pure noise from CIFAR features, something is broken.

PROS / FIT
  - Gaussian is a cheap, unambiguous far-OOD sanity check / ablation. It anchors
    the "far" end of the near-vs-far axis alongside SVHN.

CONS / CAVEAT (flagged)
  - Gaussian is TRIVIALLY easy, so it is a sanity check, NOT a discriminating
    test. The discriminating RQ3 evidence remains the near-OOD pools.
  - TinyImageNet remains UNCURATED (all 200 classes; no CIFAR-FS-overlap
    filtering -- implementation.txt 7.1 risk). RQ3's near-OOD claim therefore
    rests on cifar100_near (16 val classes, provably disjoint from the 20 test
    classes), with tin_near as a corroborating (slightly contaminated) pool.

  / FPR@95 0.093 — energy is near-ceiling on pure noise, as expected for the
  easy far end.)

4.3  RQ3 PARAGRAPH (lifts into Chapter 5)
  Evidential uncertainty (vacuity) beats softmax confidence (MSP) on EVERY OOD
  pool tested, and -- the RQ3 point -- by a LARGER margin on near-OOD than
  far-OOD. On far-OOD the gap is +0.045 AUROC (SVHN) and +0.048 (Gaussian
  noise); on near-OOD it widens to +0.098 (clean CIFAR-100-heldout) and +0.080
  (TinyImageNet). FPR@95 tells the same story more starkly: on near-OOD
  CIFAR-100 evidential rejects at 0.393 vs softmax's 0.667. This is exactly the
  expected mechanism (OpenOOD near>far difficulty; Malinin & Gales): softmax MSP
  degrades under semantic shift where the head is still confident, whereas
  Dirichlet vacuity stays discriminative. The Gaussian far-OOD sanity check
  passes -- both heads are near-ceiling on white noise (evidential 0.980;
  softmax MSP 0.932, energy 0.984) -- confirming nothing is broken at the easy
  end. Softmax energy remains competitive on far-OOD (it is a non-probabilistic
  score), but on the decisive near-OOD axis the evidential head is the clear
  winner. Net: on the best PEFT config (parallel placement), the evidential head
  buys its OOD robustness precisely where it matters -- near-OOD -- at the cost
  of the calibration gap documented in Steps 4.5/5/6.
