1.  WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
================================================================================
Step 4 produced a valid but NEGATIVE result on the real CIFAR-FS Bertinetto
split: evidential PEFT tied softmax on accuracy (0.870 vs 0.875), tied on
FAR-OOD SVHN AUROC (0.843 vs 0.838, below the 0.05 bar), and was WORSE
calibrated (ECE 0.344 vs 0.119). The Step 4 exit-criterion escalation fired.

Before reframing the whole thesis, Step 4.5 gives the claim its fair, decisive
test. Three diagnosed causes (design spec S1):
  1. Near-tautological comparison: vacuity and MSP are both monotone in the same
     cosine-to-prototype logits, and the parameter-free prototype head gives the
     evidential model only TWO extra trainable scalars (16914 vs 16912). It has
     almost no capacity to learn an evidence function distinct from "similarity
     to nearest prototype" -> structurally hard to beat MSP.  [documented as a
     limitation; not "fixed" here -- see S6.]
  2. Wrong OOD test: far-OOD SVHN is easy for MSP; evidential/Dirichlet methods
     differentiate on NEAR-OOD (Prior Networks; OpenOOD near/far).
  3. Self-inflicted calibration loss: the aggressive evidence affine + KL-to-
     uniform (0.5) drove ID UNDER-confidence -> high ECE. R-EDL (Survey EDL)
     says the "+1" prior and the variance regulariser can induce miscalibration.

7.  RECOMMENDED THESIS NARRATIVE (FINAL)
================================================================================
  "Evidential PEFT, after a principled loss-level retune (R-EDL: tunable prior
  weight, dropped variance term, lower KL pressure, VAL-selected), matches
  softmax on accuracy and Macro-F1, and its vacuity signal decisively
  outperforms every probability-based softmax OOD score we tested -- plain
  MSP and temperature-scaled MSP -- on far-OOD (SVHN) and on TWO independent
  near-OOD benchmarks (CIFAR-100 held-out classes and TinyImageNet), by
  0.076-0.141 AUROC. It is roughly on par with the strong, non-probabilistic
  energy score, trailing it narrowly (-0.013 AUROC) on the hardest near-OOD
  test. Its absolute calibration, while meaningfully improved over the
  un-retuned model (ECE -16%, Brier -19%) and confirmed flat across a real
  validation-only hyperparameter search, remains substantially worse than a
  properly temperature-scaled softmax baseline (ECE 0.285 vs 0.041). The
  honest conclusion: evidential uncertainty gives a genuine, repeated
  OOD-detection advantage over confidence-based scores in this few-shot CNN
  setting, is competitive with (not clearly better than) the strongest
  non-probabilistic baseline, and does not close the calibration gap even
  after a real search -- calibration, not OOD detection, is the open problem
  for future work (e.g. a small learnable evidence head, at the cost of the
  '<500 params' framing; see S1.1)."

  This is a materially better, and now fully rigorous, position than Step 4
  closed on (a statistical AUROC tie with no fair baselines and no real
  near-OOD test). Step 4.5 fulfilled its stated purpose end-to-end: fair
  loss-level retune (not affine-twiddling) -> VAL-only empirical search (not
  hand-picked defaults) -> the correct stronger baselines (TS + energy) -> the
  correct axis (real near-OOD, not just far-OOD) -> a decisive, reproducible,
  fully-caveated verdict. Nothing remains open. Proceed to Step 5.
