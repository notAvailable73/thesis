0.  PAPER-GROUNDED REASONING (per thesis_implementation_instructions Section 5)
================================================================================
Sources: PAPER SUMMARIES/FEW SHOTpaper_summaries.txt -- "Matching Networks for
One Shot Learning" (Vinyals et al. 2016), "Prototypical Networks for Few-shot
Learning" (Snell et al. 2017), "A Closer Look at Few-shot Classification"
(Chen et al. 2019).

WHICH PAPERS / WHY
  - Vinyals 2016 INTRODUCES MiniImageNet ("100 classes, 600 images each,
    84x84, 80 train / 20 test"). Its OWN split is not what this thesis uses --
    see the flagged gap below.
  - Neither Vinyals 2016 nor a standalone Ravi & Larochelle 2017 paper is
    separately summarised in this repo's paper_summaries.txt (only 4 few-shot
    papers were processed; Ravi & Larochelle's "Optimization as a Model for
    Few-Shot Learning" is not one of them). The 64/16/20 split this thesis
    uses is instead corroborated INDIRECTLY: Snell 2017's own summary states
    "Omniglot and miniImageNet (Ravi & Larochelle 64/16/20 splits)", and Chen
    2019's summary independently states "mini-ImageNet (64/16/20 split)" as
    its dataset. Two independent summaries citing the same split number is
    reasonable corroboration that 64/16/20 is the field's canonical split,
    but this is NOT the same as having Ravi & Larochelle's own paper summary
    to check the split's provenance against -- flagged per Section 6 (do not
    fill a gap with an invented citation). The frozen wnid list this thesis
    ships (MINI_IMAGENET_SPLIT in src/datasets/mini_imagenet.py) instead comes
    from the twitter/meta-learning-lstm repo's train/val/test.csv, verified
    live during planning to be 64/16/20 classes, pairwise disjoint, union 100.
  - Snell 2017 (Prototypical Networks) is why this step needed NO new
    trainer/evaluator code: its protocol -- 5-way, 1-/5-shot, 15 query/class,
    600 test episodes, 95% CI, Euclidean distance over cosine -- is exactly
    what src/trainers/episodic_trainer.py + src/evaluators/episodic.py already
    implement for CIFAR-FS (Phase 2 / Step 4). MiniImageNet slots into the
    same PrototypeHead + episode-sampler contract with zero changes to either.
  - Chen 2019 ("A Closer Look") directly grounds the Step 9 linear-probe
    reference runs: its "Baseline" (freeze the feature extractor, train a
    fresh classifier on the novel-class support set) is presented as the
    natural non-meta-learning reference point, and the paper's central,
    counter-intuitive finding is that deeper backbones SHRINK the gap between
    this simple baseline and sophisticated meta-learners. This is the exact
    mechanism behind Step 9's own confound (Section 5): MiniImageNet classes
    were seen during ImageNet pretraining, so the frozen ResNet-18/
    MobileNetV3-Small features already encode most of what a 5-way classifier
    needs, and the adapter's marginal contribution should be expected to
    shrink relative to CIFAR-FS. This is exactly the "simpler baseline can be
    surprisingly competitive" pattern thesis_implementation_instructions.txt
    Section 4 names as something to watch for, not dismiss -- the
    linear-probe reference runs exist specifically to measure it rather than
    just assert it.

PROS / CONS / FIT (thesis_implementation_instructions.txt Section 3)
  PROS: a second canonical in-distribution dataset gives RQ2 (calibration)
    and RQ3 (near-OOD) genuine cross-dataset breadth instead of resting on
    one benchmark; the linear-probe uplift number Chen 2019 motivates turns a
    known confound into a measured quantity; the entire episodic
    trainer/evaluator/OOD-scoring stack is reused unchanged (Snell 2017's
    protocol was already implemented).
  CONS: none of the source papers anticipated frozen-ImageNet-pretrained
    backbones being evaluated on ImageNet-derived novel classes -- the
    "novel classes already seen in pretraining" confound is a consequence of
    THIS thesis's specific choice (ImageNet-pretrained CNN backbones), not
    something any few-shot paper's protocol accounts for. It is flagged
    honestly in Section 5, not treated as a defect in the papers.
  FIT: still squarely serves the thesis's actual goals (few-shot CNN
    adaptation + parameter efficiency + calibrated uncertainty + OOD
    robustness) -- proposal Section 6 and implementation.txt Section 9 both
    name MiniImageNet as required breadth, not an optional extension.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE PAPERS / FROM THE CIFAR-FS PATTERN, AND WHY
  - No `class_ids=` override on get_mini_imagenet (CIFAR-FS has one, kept for
    Step 1-3 backward-compat). Every MiniImageNet source layout (the Zenodo
    pkl caches, an images/+csv mirror, a wnid-imagefolder mirror) is already
    split by construction -- there is no single shared upstream Dataset to
    filter by class id the way CIFAR-100 is for CIFAR-FS. Not a paper-driven
    decision; a consequence of the data's actual shape.
  - The Zenodo pkl schema this loader assumes (`{"image_data": uint8 array,
    "class_dict": {wnid: indices}}`) is a DATA-ENGINEERING fact (the de facto
    learn2learn/MAML++ convention), not something any paper_summaries.txt
    entry states -- thesis_implementation_instructions.txt's paper-grounding
    process does not apply to it. The loader validates the schema at load
    time and raises with the observed keys/shape rather than silently
    guessing if a staged file doesn't match (see
    src/datasets/mini_imagenet.py:_load_pkl_cache).
  - The TinyImageNet-wnid-exclusion decision (Section 5 below) is an
    engineering response to a data fact discovered during planning (25 shared
    wnids, 5 of them MiniImageNet TEST classes) -- not derived from any paper;
    documented as a Step 9 decision in progress.txt's decisions log.

KNOWN LIMITATION / UNTESTED ASSUMPTION CARRIED INTO THE CODE
  - The pkl-schema assumption above has not been exercised against a real
    downloaded file in this session (no network-capable Python environment
    was available with torch/pickle-friendly numpy). The first real run
    (notebooks/step9_mini.ipynb, Section 5 "cache build") is also the first
    real test of this code path end-to-end.
  - The recipe (LR, rank, KL weight, evidence affine) is inherited UNCHANGED
    from the ResNet-18 CIFAR-FS VAL sweep (Step 4.5), exactly as Step 8 did
    for MobileNetV3 -- deliberately, to isolate the dataset axis, at the cost
    of confounding "MiniImageNet numbers" with "recipe never re-tuned for
    MiniImageNet". Per-dataset VAL re-tuning is Step 10's job.

5.  HONEST CAVEATS
================================================================================
  - NOT YET RUN. Every number a reader might expect in Section 4 is absent by
    design -- see STATUS at the top. Do not treat this file as reporting a
    result until that section is filled from a real run.
  - IMAGENET-PRETRAINING OVERLAP (the central caveat for this whole step).
    MiniImageNet's 100 classes are a subset of ILSVRC-2012, and both frozen
    backbones are ImageNet-pretrained -- every "novel" MiniImageNet test
    class was seen during pretraining. This does not invalidate the
    within-dataset evidential-vs-softmax comparison (both heads share the
    same backbone), but it makes MiniImageNet-vs-CIFAR-FS ABSOLUTE accuracy
    comparison meaningless without the linear-probe uplift control (Section
    0, Section 4.3c).
  - TinyImageNet-wnid-filtering makes Step 9's tin_near NOT directly
    comparable to Steps 4.5/7/8's tin_near, even for the reused CIFAR-FS
    rows in the Section 1 consolidation table -- those CIFAR-FS numbers use
    exclude_wnids=None (byte-identical to their original run); only the NEW
    mini_imagenet tin_near numbers are filtered. Comparing "tin_near" across
    the two datasets in the same table column is comparing two different
    500-image samples drawn under two different exclusion rules -- state
    this explicitly if the thesis text puts them side by side.
  - SINGLE SEED. All 10 runs are seed 42 only, same limitation Steps 5-8
    carry. The 600-episode CI95 is over episodes, not over training seeds.
  - RECIPE NOT RE-TUNED FOR MINIIMAGENET. LR, rank, KL weight, evidence
    affine are inherited unchanged from the ResNet-18 CIFAR-FS VAL sweep
    (Step 4.5), deliberately (isolates the dataset axis) but confounding any
    "MiniImageNet result" with "recipe never tuned for this dataset". Belongs
    to Step 10 to fix, exactly as Step 8 flagged for MobileNetV3.
  - THE ZENODO PKL SCHEMA ASSUMPTION IS UNTESTED AGAINST REAL DATA IN THIS
    SESSION. src/datasets/mini_imagenet.py:_load_pkl_cache assumes the
    {"image_data", "class_dict"} schema (the community learn2learn/MAML++
    convention); this was validated by unit test against SYNTHETIC pickles
    only (tests/test_mini_imagenet.py), because no environment with torch/
    numpy/PIL was available to fetch and inspect the real ~1.8 GB files. The
    notebook's Section 5 cache-build cell is the first real exercise of this
    path; if it raises the "unrecognized MiniImageNet cache format" error
    from _load_pkl_cache, read the printed keys/shape and adjust the loader
    -- do not silently guess a different schema.
  - Only Bottleneck (post_pool + parallel) + Linear-Probe are RUN on
    MiniImageNet. LoRA / BitFit / Full-FT are wired and tested against
    CIFAR-FS/MobileNetV3 (Steps 5/8) but not re-run here --
    implementation.txt Section 9's scope is Bottleneck x {evidential,
    softmax}; the full cross-product (incl. LoRA/BitFit/Full-FT on
    MiniImageNet) is Step 10's / Step 12.F's job.
  - No change was made that alters any Steps-1-8 result: the dispatch
